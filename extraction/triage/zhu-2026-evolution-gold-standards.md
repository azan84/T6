# zhu-2026-evolution-gold-standards

## Bibliographic
- Title: The Evolution of Gold Standards in Coronary Artery Disease Diagnosis: From Coronary Angiography (CAG) to Fractional Flow Reserve (FFR) and Quantitative Flow Ratio (QFR)
- First author / authors (first 3 + et al.): Yanan Dai, Pengxiong Zhu, Jun Liu et al.
- Year: 2026
- Venue: SSRN (preprint)
- DOI: NOT AVAILABLE (preprint ssrn-6780918)

## One-line claim
Narrative review traces the clinical evolution from anatomical CAG assessment through physiological FFR to computational QFR, highlighting diagnostic gaps, procedural barriers, and evidence supporting non-invasive FFR-like modalities.
