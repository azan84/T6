# maier-hein-2020-bias-guidelines

## Bibliographic
- Title: BIAS: Transparent reporting of biomedical image analysis challenges
- First author / authors: Lena Maier-Hein, Annika Reinke, Michal Kozubek
- Year: 2020
- Venue: Medical Image Analysis, vol. 66
- DOI: 10.1016/j.media.2020.101796

## One-line claim
A standardized reporting guideline for biomedical image analysis challenges developed by 49 scientists across 30 institutions; presents a 42-parameter checklist to improve transparency, reproducibility and interpretability of challenge design and results across all image modalities and task categories.
