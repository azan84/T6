# chaparro-cardenas-2025-digital-twins

## Bibliographic
- Title: A Technological Review of Digital Twins and Artificial Intelligence for Personalized and Predictive Healthcare
- First author / authors (first 3 + et al.): Chaparro-Cárdenas SL, Ramirez-Bautista J-A, Terven J
- Year: 2025
- Venue: Healthcare 13:1763
- DOI: 10.3390/healthcare13141763

## One-line claim
Digital twins integrated with AI and IoT enable real-time patient monitoring and personalized intervention across rehabilitation, diagnostics, and drug development, though clinical validation and scalability to diverse populations remain unresolved.

## T6 targeted questions
- **Q-A geometry perturbation**: Not specifically addressed. Discussion of geometry change in rehabilitation (robotic arm trajectory control) but no segmentation-error or inter-observer disagreement quantification.
- **Q-B decision flip**: Not reported.
- **Q-C BC tuning**: Not directly addressed. Paper discusses continuous recalibration of DT models via incoming wearable and sensor data: "The integration of DTs with IoT and AI further refines predictive analytics, enabling early detection of impending health issues."
- **Q-D fidelity / quantity**: Both 3D simulation and reduced-order modeling mentioned (e.g., knee-strength exoskeleton with DT framework; robot-assisted surgery DT prototype). WSS/OSI not mentioned.
- **Q-E data**: Multiple applications cited without specific cohort sizes or FFR data. Telemedicine and remote rehabilitation contexts emphasized.
- **Q-F meshing**: Not reported.
