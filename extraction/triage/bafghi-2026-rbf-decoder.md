# bafghi-2026-rbf-decoder

## Bibliographic
- Title: From Centerlines to Hemodynamics: Anisotropic RBF Decoders for Coronary Arteries
- First author / authors (first 3 + et al.): Reza Akbarian Bafghi, Sukirt Thakur, Maziar Raissi
- Year: 2026
- Venue: Transactions on Machine Learning Research
- DOI: arXiv:2605.27578v2

## One-line claim
A Transformer-Anisotropic RBF neural operator learns to predict continuous pressure and wall shear stress fields on coronary artery walls from vessel centerline geometry and inlet flow, trained on synthetic and multi-vessel OpenFOAM CFD data.

## T6 targeted questions
- **Q-A geometry perturbation**: Two datasets: (i) synthetic benchmark of 4,200 single-vessel geometries with "controlled anatomical variations" (researcher-chosen), and (ii) multi-vessel dataset of 4,800 cases "generated by randomly introducing stenoses and varying physiologically plausible flow rates." The perturbations are SYNTHETIC (controlled and random), not real inter-observer/inter-segmenter disagreement. No measurement of actual segmentation uncertainty.
- **Q-B decision flip**: FFR estimation is mentioned as an application ("enabling FFR estimation from imaging") but the paper does NOT report FFR prediction accuracy or decision-flip/reclassification analysis. Focus is on machine learning architecture and pressure/WSS field prediction.
- **Q-C BC tuning**: The model is trained and evaluated entirely on synthetic CFD data (OpenFOAM simulations). "It serves as a fast surrogate for the simulation pipeline rather than a replacement for clinical measurement." No discussion of boundary condition tuning, re-tuning, or whether learned surrogate absorbs/compensates for geometric error.
- **Q-D fidelity / quantity**: The surrogate learns from 3D CFD (OpenFOAM Navier-Stokes) simulations to predict continuous pressure and wall shear stress (WSS) fields. Does NOT solve 3D CFD directly; provides mesh-free, fast inference of pressure and WSS at arbitrary wall query points. Inference cost nearly invariant to query-point count.
- **Q-E data**: Synthetic: 4,200 single-vessel geometries. Multi-vessel: 4,800 cases from ImageCAS dataset spanning left and right coronary arteries. Single-vessel dataset publicly available.
- **Q-F meshing**: The method is mesh-free; uses centerline + radius field encoding via transformer and anisotropic RBF kernel decoder. "Continuous, mesh-independent field reconstruction" is a key contribution. Does NOT require volumetric or surface mesh discretization.

## Novelty bearing on T6
- bucket: METHOD
- one-line reason: Demonstrates fast machine-learning surrogate for coronary hemodynamics without expensive 3D CFD or explicit mesh generation; relevant to T6's computational efficiency, though uses synthetic perturbations and trained surrogate rather than first-principles BC tuning.
- verdict: LIGHT
- revisit-if: If paper or related work validates the surrogate on real patient cohorts with invasive FFR, or explores how segmentation uncertainty propagates through the learned operator to affect pressure/WSS/FFR predictions.
