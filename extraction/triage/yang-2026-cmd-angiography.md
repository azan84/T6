# yang-2026-cmd-angiography

## Bibliographic
- Title: Assessing coronary microvascular dysfunction using angiography-based data-driven methods
- First author / authors (first 3 + et al.): Haizhou Yang, Jiyang Zhang, Brahmajee K. Nallamothu, et al.
- Year: 2026
- Venue: Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering 452 (2026) 118743
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cma.2026.118743

## One-line claim
Develops a data-driven framework using neural networks to predict coronary microvascular dysfunction (IMR and CFR) indices from dynamic contrast intensity profiles in computational angiography.

## T6 targeted questions

- **Q-A geometry perturbation**: Does NOT perturb segmentation. Uses synthetic angiograms generated by physics-based CFD model, not real segmentation uncertainty.

- **Q-B decision flip**: NOT REPORTED. Paper focuses on CMD indices (IMR, CFR) not FFR threshold decision-making. One example mentions "FFR = 8,973/12,491 = 0.72" in epicardial disease case but no systematic analysis of 0.80 threshold reclassification.

- **Q-C BC tuning**: YES. Uses 3D-0D coupling with Windkessel at aortic outlet: "The aortic outlet is connected to an aorta LPM, defined via a three-element Windkessel model [32], consisting of systemic compliance Cs, proximal systemic resistance Rsp, and distal systemic resistance Rsd." Does NOT report re-tuning after geometry change or compensation for error. Assumes uniform microvascular parameters.

- **Q-D fidelity / quantity**: Uses 3D-0D multi-physics model with Navier-Stokes and contrast transport. Reports CFR and IMR indices. Does NOT report WSS or OSI. Authors note: "we estimated average indices for all vessels of the tree. Therefore, we are providing a characterization of global microvascular health, rather than individual branches."

- **Q-E data**: Single patient-specific geometry (64-year-old female with suspected CMD), synthetic data generation only. No invasive ground truth.

- **Q-F meshing**: Limited detail. Mentions computational domain coupling to LPMs but no discussion of meshing robustness on poor geometry. Focuses on synthetic data generation.

## Novelty bearing on T6

- bucket: BACKGROUND
- one-line reason: Provides context on data-driven CFD methods for CMD but does not address segmentation error, BC compensation, or topological error effects.
- verdict: LIGHT
- revisit-if: Paper validates framework on clinical angiograms with ground truth; analyzes segmentation perturbation impact on CMD predictions.
