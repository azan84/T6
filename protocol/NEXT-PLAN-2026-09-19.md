   > a 3D U-Net which predicted the same boundaries for unedited centerlines, and therefore the DSC inter-observer

## 2. What (2) actually does to the paper — read this before rewording anything

**Direction first, because it is easy to get backwards.** Higher DSC = more agreement. "Upper bound of agreement"
means two genuinely independent annotators would agree **less**. So the measured disagreement is a **lower bound on
real disagreement**, and therefore:

> **Every injected magnitude in `error_types.py` is conservative.** T4's 7 % radius under-read (from DSC 92.8 %),
> T3's 2.46 mm HD95, and the T1/T2 topological rate are all *floors*. Any decision flip we demonstrate at these
> magnitudes would occur at least as often with independently produced segmentations.

That is an asset, not a wound — **provided we say it ourselves, first, and cite the dataset author's own sentence.**
It converts reviewer attack 3 ("the injected errors are unrealistic") from a defence into an offensive point: the
errors are *under*-stated, and the dataset's own authors say so in print.

> **CORRECTED 2026-09-19 after independent audit** (`references/FABLE-AUDIT-RESULTS-PLAN-2026-09-19.md` §B1–B3).
> The first draft of this section over-generalised in two ways and both are fixed below: the floor argument does
> **not** hold magnitude-by-magnitude, and the reason for withdrawing C1's topological claim was wrong.

**The floor argument holds for the statistics, not for every magnitude.** Per-magnitude:

| magnitude | does "shared initialisation ⇒ conservative" transfer? |
|---|---|
| **T4** radius scale 0.930, from DSC 92.8 % | **No, direction unknown.** The DSC→k conversion attributes the *whole* overlap deficit to coaxial calibre, which is an **upper** bound on the calibre share — the opposite bias. Net effect unresolved. |
| **T3** 2.46 mm, from HD95 | **No.** HD95 is dominated by topological discrepancies and never measured lesion axial extent. No direction transfers. |
| **T2** 15 mm truncation | **N/A — not a measured magnitude.** It is chosen. |
| **T1** largest downstream branch | **N/A — not a measured magnitude**, and it deletes the *largest* branch while the dataset's ρ = +0.89 says real disagreement sits in the *smallest*. |

There is also an **opposing bias the first draft ignored**: Bransby says the second set was deliberately left
unchecked *so that variability would not be under-estimated*. That pushes the measured disagreement up, not down.
**So the honest statement is narrow:** where a magnitude derives from an agreement statistic, shared initialisation
biases that statistic toward agreement. It does not license "every injected magnitude is conservative", and it must
not be written that way. It also touches **H1**, whose premise is "at matched disagreement magnitude" — reword or
declare.

**C1's topological claim must still be withdrawn, but for a different and much sharper reason.** ImageCAS-X reports
**Betti error = β₀ + β₁** — connected components and independent loops. **A missed side branch changes neither**: the
tree remains one component and acquires no cycle. So the 0.2 ± 0.4 figure never measured T1 at all. It *is* a genuine
measurement of **vessel breaks** (which raise β₀), so it continues to support **T2**.

This matters because **T1 is the paper's main effect** and supplies ≥ 85 % of the detector's positive class. The
replacement wording is not "a floor measured under shared-centreline initialisation" (the first draft's proposal,
which is still wrong) but: *Betti error bounds the break rate (T2); no published statistic in this dataset measures
the missed-branch rate, and T1's magnitude is a design choice, declared as such.*

**What offer (3) is actually for — and it is not a new experimental arm.** The first draft claimed model predictions
give "the genuine topological rate C1 needs". They do not: model-vs-official is a **model-error** regime (β-err 1.9
CAS-Net, 5.6 nnU-Net, against 0.2 inter-observer — ten to forty times more topologically wrong). Different estimand,
and the paper must never present it as inter-observer variability.

Its real value is the thing `STATISTICS-PLAN` §13 **already declares as a weakness**: **error-type apportionment**.
Across many scans and several models it measures which branches go missing and at what calibre, how often vessels
break, and how radius bias varies with diameter — the mix our injection design currently *assumes*. Used to calibrate
and justify that apportionment, it strengthens C1 directly.

**And "A/B/C on model-vs-official with no injection" cannot stand.** It contradicts E0: the natural cohort has
**2 trees ≤ 0.80 and 4 in 0.75–0.85** — that is the entire reason the severity sweep exists. A no-injection arm has
almost no decision-relevant instances and H7's flip table would be empty. Two viable designs, and one must be chosen
and registered:

- **(a)** insert the *same* virtual stenoses into **both** anatomies — real topological/calibre error plus controlled
  severity. A good design, but a **new** one: it needs its own eligibility rules on the model-predicted tree.
- **(b)** keep it no-injection and make the endpoint **ΔFFR only**, with no flip claim.

**Framing correction:** "an FFR-CT pipeline is fed an automatic segmentation" is not how regulated FFR-CT is
deployed — the segmentation is analyst-edited before flow computation. Raw model output is the **pre-QA** input, and
the paper must say pre-QA. Also: model-vs-official differences include the official mask's **own** error; it is a
checked annotation, not ground truth, and that must be stated.

Draft: `drafts/EMAIL-IMAGECAS-X-REPLY-2026-09-19.md` (v2, incorporating the audit). Accept, thank him for the
caveat, tell him we are withdrawing the Betti-based claim and why, and ask four questions:

1. **How many scans, and which models** — his *"a distribution of predictions or a single scan"* is ambiguous and the

2. **Raw or post-processed — and can we have both.** The benchmark removed components **below ~100 voxels**, not LCC.

   are clean; at least one public CAS-Net checkpoint used a different split and may have seen these scans.
4. **Terms** — licence, citation, redeposit of derived results as Source Data, and preferred attribution including
   co-authorship.

Plus one optional: the **pre-edit automatic centrelines**, which would give a measured topological *correction* rate
and separate centreline error from lumen error.

**Dropped from the first draft:** asking him to confirm the shared-initialisation caveat extends to the topological
statistics. His confirmation cannot repair a statistic that does not measure the event (§2).
