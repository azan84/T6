## Question
The lesion80 result (converged at iteration 1364, mesh `mesh_case2`, 2,682,675 cells, 50 um cells in the tube around the LAD s=17.5-36 mm and the first 10.5 mm of D1, 3 boundary layers ratio 1.2) is a single-mesh result. Which of its quantities are insensitive to the throat resolution, which are not? Nothing else changes (same STL, BCs, numerics, 0D twin).

## Flagged-cell rule (revised before the solves)
The lesion80 acceptance rule (no flagged entity inside the 3 mm tube around the LAD for s=18.5-32 mm) is met by M50 (nearest 6.1 mm) and W100 (nearest 6.3 mm), failed by the rejected T100, and CANNOT be met by any 25 um zone inside a 50 um tube (a size-jump interface lies inside the analysis window). Positions are recorded per mesh (`failed_sets_localisation.json`, vertices with arc/axis-distance/branch; vertex counts in the tube by set concave-cells/concave-faces/low-quality-tet-faces): T25a 10/117/106 with arc histogram at s=20-21 and 26-27 (its zone edges 20.5/26.5); T25b 61/110/45 at s=18-20 and 27-29 (its zone edges 19.0/28.0) and none at 20.3-21 or 26.5. (The earlier design's "233 / 201 points" double-counted cell centres and vertices.) Replacement rule: the empirical interface check - a quantity is only called insensitive if T25a and T25b, whose interfaces are at different arcs on BOTH sides, agree within the tolerance; disagreement = INTERFACE-CONTAMINATED. Limits, stated: different interface positions remove the identified shared-location blind spot but do not prove the absence of a bias common to any 25 um/50 um interface, and T25b also resolves more volume at 25 um than T25a, so a disagreement cannot uniquely be attributed to an interface (it can equally be a resolved-volume effect); the label names the observation, not a proven cause. T25a-vs-T25b disagreement therefore also includes the difference in zone extent and is reported as "not insensitive (interface or extent)". Where a quantity is beyond the tolerance from M50 (SENSITIVE) the label precedence below applies before the interface label.
`mesh_failed_sets_general.py` now fails on an empty set search whenever the strict log reports failures; M50 evidence is regenerated (`lesion80/mesh_case2/failed_sets_localisation.json`, 0 vertices in the tube).

Predefined criteria (analyze_solve.py): p and U residuals < 1e-5, every outlet flux and pressure stable < 0.1 % over 200 iterations, mass < 0.1 %, BC error < 0.1 %, monitors aligned. Its role, correctly stated: the reference PRESSURE loss cancels from differences of the added static loss between lesion levels; the reference FLOW does not cancel from the percentage flow-reduction differences (difference of two levels = 100 (Q_b - Q_a)/Q_ref) - it is their normalisation, so the 0.5 pp criterion presumes the healthy flows are level-independent (supported only by the 1.1 M vs 3.25 M comparison above); the M50 value sets the scale of the relative tolerances.

## Quantities, stations and pre-registered rules (constants in `mesh_sensitivity.py`, fixed BEFORE any of these solves; engineering judgement, not from the study spec; not changed after seeing results)
Pressure-loss stations are the sections at s=18.5 and s=29.5 mm (29.5 is the last 0.25 mm-spaced section; there is no section at 30.0 mm - the earlier design, the code and one manuscript table used "30.0" but the value came from 29.5; the manuscript is corrected; lookups now require the exact station). Section grid: 0.25 mm from 17.5 to 29.5 then 31.0, 32.5, 34.0, 35.5, 37.0, 38.5, 40.0, 41.5; WSS grid 0.25 mm from 15 to 45.
| quantity | definition | tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| flow_red_distal_pct | 100 (1 - (Q_D1+Q_D2+Q_LAD)/(same in solve_baseline_ref)) | 0.5 pp |
| flow_red_total_pct | same for the total outflow | 0.5 pp |
| added_static_loss_mmHg | (P(18.5)-P(29.5)) lesion minus the same in the reference, section-mean static P, mmHg | 3 % of the M50 value |
| p_min_over_Pao | minimum section-mean static P/P_aorta over the sections 22.0-29.5 | 0.0033 (0.3 mmHg) |
| umax_ms | maximum axial velocity over the sections 22.0-26.0 | 5 % |
| tau_peak_throat_Pa | maximum circumferential-mean forward-positive wall shear over stations 22.0-25.0 | 5 % |
| onset_arc_mm | first station at/after the throat (23.5) with reversed-wall fraction >= 1 %, needs EVERY station 23.5-45 | 0.25 mm |
| recovery_arc_wss_mm | first station after the onset from which the fraction stays < 1 % through 45 mm (JUNCTION-INFLUENCED, outside the refined tube: resolution-limited) | 1 mm |
| max_reversed_wall_fraction | maximum over WSS stations 24.0-39.0 | 0.05 |
| max_reversed_area_fraction | maximum over the sections 24.0-38.5 | 0.05 |
fine = X(T25a) - X(M50); interface = X(T25a) - X(T25b); coarse = X(W100) - X(M50); "exceeds" means beyond the tolerance by more than 1e-9 relative (equality is within tolerance).
Label precedence: UNASSESSABLE > SENSITIVE > INTERFACE-CONTAMINATED > NON-MONOTONE > INSENSITIVE.
UNASSESSABLE: any needed value missing (a station absent, failing validation or non-finite; windows are NEVER shrunk), non-finite, or any of REF/M50/T25a/T25b unconverged or with a final iteration different from the reconstructed time read by the post-processors; if only W100 is unconverged, monotonicity is not assessed ("INSENSITIVE on the fine side").
SENSITIVE: |T25a - M50| or |T25b - M50| exceeds the tolerance. INTERFACE-CONTAMINATED: both within the tolerance of M50 but |T25a - T25b| exceeds it. NON-MONOTONE: |W100 - M50| exceeds the tolerance and (M50 - T25a) has the opposite sign from (W100 - M50) with magnitude > 0.2 tolerance (the series W100 -> M50 -> T25a is in increasing resolution; monotone = the two successive differences have the same sign; the earlier prose had the signs inverted, the code was right). INSENSITIVE otherwise. Physical outcomes are not missing data: onset has the categories separated / none, recovery recovered / not_recovered / no_separation; a category without a real arc takes the sentinel 50.0 mm with a recorded note for the arithmetic, but the CATEGORIES are compared first: if they differ among M50, T25a and T25b the label is SENSITIVE (outcome category differs), never UNASSESSABLE and never masked by the sentinel. A category difference of W100 alone (fine side unchanged) is not SENSITIVE by itself - the same as a large monotone W100 change of a numeric quantity, coarse-side dependence is allowed - and is labelled "INSENSITIVE on the fine side; W100 outcome category differs", with the two categories stated. The study "passes" for a quantity only with INSENSITIVE; the other labels are findings and the corresponding lesion80 number is then qualified in the manuscript. Outlet pressure ratios are reported without a rule. No GCI.
`test_mesh_sensitivity.py` (97 checks; 32 unit-level: monotonicity direction incl. the audit's example, threshold equality and just-above, T25b-only exceedance, interface disagreement, precedence, NaN/+-inf, unconverged W100, relative tolerance with a zero reference, and real-data mutations: NaN or missing WSS after 39 mm, dropped stations, inf, invalid or absent 29.5 section, missing far section; 12 main()-level checks on copied case directories: identical inputs, W100 converged-but-time-inconsistent -> everything UNASSESSABLE (never excused as unconverged), W100 unconverged with absent post-processing -> fine-side labels only, converged W100 without post-processing -> hard error, reference infinite/zero/missing/null flows -> UNASSESSABLE (no exception, no spurious 100 % reduction), ok=NaN at 29.5, unconverged T25a, non-positive equal times) all pass. The registered W100 exception is reserved for a genuinely unconverged W100; `ok` flags must be explicitly True.

## Post-processing robustness (fixed since round 1)
`lesion_sections.py` and `mesh_local_verification.py` now triangulate and clean the slice before connectivity (a plain slice returned mismatched-length arrays and an empty "closest" part at some planes of T25b, e.g. s=19.0, giving a spurious 100 % section-area error); the finished M50 and reference sections regenerated with the new code differ by <= 2.7e-5 relative (area) and 1e-9 (pressure), flags identical; `lesion_sections.py` writes the read time next to each CSV; `mesh_sensitivity.py` requires analysis, sections and WSS to share the final iteration.
Mesh checks on the final meshes: section-area mesh vs STL at 59 stations s=17.5-32: max |error| M50 0.34 %, T25a 0.076 %, T25b 0.076 %, W100 2.01 % (throat only; the sole station above 2 %).

## What this study cannot show (stated in the manuscript)
Convergence of the recirculation extent from above; sensitivity of the healthy reference; independence from the STL facet size (0.017-0.24 mm edges in the window; identical geometry in all levels); turbulence/unsteadiness (steady laminar solver); an interface-free fine mesh; an uncertainty bound of any kind.
