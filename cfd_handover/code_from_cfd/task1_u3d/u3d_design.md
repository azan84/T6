## 1. Question
Successive 3D FFR differences of sten70 at x = 56.5 mm did not shrink in the earlier four-level ladder (Section 11.3 of the report: GCI not trustworthy), and that ladder held the boundary-layer stack constant, so it was not a single-parameter refinement family. Return ONE number, U3D (the 3D FFR discretisation uncertainty of sten70), with its defining sentence, from a systematic family: 3D cfMesh levels with throat-zone cell sizes 50 / 25 / 12.5 um (ratio exactly 2), boundary-layer stack scaling with the cell size, plus a 2D axisymmetric wedge family (>= 4 levels, ratio 2) as the physics-versus-discretisation cross-check.

## 2. Geometry (identical for every 3D level)
Analytic sten70 of make_stageA_geometry.py (radius law radius_sten, 1.8 -> 0.9 mm taper over x = 0..90 mm, straight extensions x = -15..0 and 90..100 mm, 10 mm cosine lesion centred at x = 31.5 mm, 70 % diameter stenosis: throat radius 0.4455 mm, diameter 0.891 mm). The Stage A STL (64 circumferential segments, 0.1 mm axial rings) is too coarse for 12.5 um cells (facets of 100-180 um), so ONE finer STL is written for all levels by P/u3d/make_sten70_fine_stl.py: same radius law and the generator's own write_stl (outward normals, solids inlet/outlet/wall), 192 circumferential segments, axial ring spacing 0.02 mm for x in [20, 43] and 0.2 mm elsewhere. Gates: closed, one part, no illegal triangles (surfaceCheck), analytic-vs-STL throat area within 0.05 %.

## 6. Decision rules (verbatim from the work order, pre-registered here)
- 3D successive differences shrink and the finest pair is < 0.005 -> PASS; U3D from the Celik et al. (2008) GCI on the three 3D levels (h = 50, 25, 12.5 um, r = 2, observed order p = |ln|e32/e21||/ln 2 with e21 = f(S25) - f(S12), e32 = f(S50) - f(S25); GCI21 = 1.25 |e21/f(S12)| / (r^p - 1); monotone convergence required, otherwise the GCI is not applicable and the finest-pair difference is reported); those settings become the study recipe.
- 3D differences still flat but the wedge converges cleanly -> the 3D drift is a meshing artefact; report both; U3D = the 3D finest-pair difference (not the wedge GCI) with a described cause.
- Both flat -> time-accurate pimpleFoam on the finest 3D mesh with the transient BC; time-average FFR over the last stable window with mean and band; stop there.
- In every case return ONE number, U3D, with the sentence that defines it; if U3D >= 0.005 say so plainly (pre-registered rule waits on it in the statistics plan).
Zone families: the coarse level S50 is shared. Zone A = (S50, S25A, S12A), zone B = (S50, S25B, S12B; required by the work order). Per family Z: e21 = f(S25Z) - f(S12Z) (finest pair), e32 = f(S50) - f(S25Z); "shrinks" means |e21| < |e32|. The differences S12A - S12B and S25A - S25B measure the interface effect and are reported. See section 11 (v2.1) for the exact classification that turns these into rule 1, 2 or 3 and into the ONE number U3D.

## 9. Threats to validity
Zone-only refinement (not a uniform family): FFR error also has a contribution from the 50 um region downstream (jet, recovery) that does not change between levels, so U3D is a throat-zone discretisation uncertainty, not a total one; strict-mesh-check failures accepted on local evidence (as for the coronary meshes); steady laminar solutions at throat Re ~ 430 may not converge (sten80 stalled at Re ~ 400; sten70 converged in the earlier ladder); one geometry; the BL scaling is cfMesh's, verified by measurement; cfMesh quantisation forces ratio 2.

Pre-registered classification (v2.1, replaces rules iii/iv of v2 after pre-run audit round 1: the earlier wording let a noise band trigger rule 3 and demoted the Celik GCI; both contradicted the work order). Fixed here BEFORE any run.
(i) FFR of a level = last-100 mean of p_mean(x = 56.5 mm)/P_aorta at the final iteration of the fixed budget; band = max - min over the same 100 iterations. (ii) A level whose strict verdict is UNCONVERGED is reported as such and enters the differences with that flag (the fixed budget stops the run; the verdict does not).
(iii) Per zone family Z (A always; B when run): finest = |f(S12Z) - f(S25Z)|, shrinks = finest < |f(S25Z) - f(S50)|, noise_limited = max(band(S12Z), band(S25Z)) > 0.25 x finest. A family is FLAT when it does not shrink or is noise_limited. Wedge (W0..W3, W4 if run): clean = successive |differences| shrink at every step AND the Celik GCI on the three finest levels is applicable (monotone) AND the finest wedge pair < 0.005; otherwise the wedge is FLAT.
(iv) Outcome, in this order: RULE 1 (PASS): every family shrinks, none is noise-limited and every finest < 0.005 -> U3D = max over families of the Celik GCI21 in absolute FFR units (GCI21 = 1.25 |e21| / (2^p - 1), observed order p from e32/e21; a family whose GCI is not applicable [non-monotone] uses its finest pair instead, said explicitly); these production settings become the study recipe. RULE 2: some family is FLAT and the wedge is clean -> U3D = max over families of the 3D finest pair (NOT the wedge GCI), reported with the wedge result and a described cause (interfaces, cfMesh transition cells, zone-only refinement vs the uniform wedge family). RULE 3: some family is FLAT and the wedge is FLAT -> run the time-accurate pimpleFoam on the finest 3D mesh, time-average, report mean and band, stop there (U3D is then the transient result, defined then). RULE 1b (not in the work order's text, stated here): every family shrinks and none is noise-limited, but some finest >= 0.005 -> U3D = max over families of (GCI21 if applicable else finest pair) and it is reported plainly as U3D >= 0.005 with no PASS claim.
