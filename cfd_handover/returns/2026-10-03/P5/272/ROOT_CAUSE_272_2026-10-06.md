# P5 scan 272: why outlet out_396 was lost, and what the self-intersection flag is (2026-10-06)

Read-only diagnosis of the returned case (no surface, mesh or case was changed; nothing re-solved).
Scripts: `neck272.py`, `selfint272.py`, `subpatch272.py` (code_from_cfd/task_1003/p5_diagnostics/).
Raw outputs: `neck272.json`, `selfint272_R0.6mm.json`, `selfint272_R3mm.json`.

## 1. out_396 lost in the mesh: cause

Chain of causes, each measured:

1. **The mask lumen of the distal R-PDA is about two voxels wide, with a narrowing about 6.4 mm upstream of the
   outlet.** Voxel size 0.348 x 0.348 x 0.5 mm. At tree nodes 379-382 (arc 122.6-123.8 mm, 6.4 mm from the
   out_396 loop centroid) the EDT radius of the edited mask is 0.35-0.49 mm, i.e. one voxel; the package
   r_target is 0.42 mm (the tree's minimum r_target is at node 380).
2. **Marching cubes already pinches there, and the Taubin smoothing (30 iterations, passband 0.1, the 837
   recipe) narrows it further.** Section area-equivalent radius at node 381: raw marching cubes 0.339 mm ->
   smoothed 0.278 mm (-18 %), final STL 0.278 mm (inscribed radius 0.228 mm). Neighbouring nodes 380/382: raw
   0.430/0.381 -> smoothed 0.342/0.346 mm. Upstream and downstream of the neck (nodes 370-378, 383-392) raw and
   smoothed agree within about 0.04 mm. Smoothing shrinks this short, sharply curved one-voxel neck but not the
   regular vessel.
3. **No refinement object covers the neck, so cfMesh meshes it with its 200 um root cells.** meshDict:
   `maxCellSize 0.00020` (octree level 10, 0.2 mm); refinement objects are the lesion-path cones (40/70/140 um
   requests, radius 3 mm, on the RCA/R-PLA lesion path only) and 4 mm spheres at the inlet and outlet caps.
   The neck point lies in none of them (computed for all 104 objects); the out_396 cap sphere centre is 9.6 mm
   away (radius 4 mm). The inscribed diameter of the neck (0.456 mm) is therefore 2.3 root cells; its section
   area (0.243 mm2) is about six root-cell faces.
4. **cfMesh disconnects the region beyond the neck and deletes it.** `log.cartesianMesh`: "Mesh has 2 unconnected
   regions", "New cells size 5510458" after 5542985 (32,527 cells removed = the distal R-PDA with out_396),
   so out_396 has 0 faces.

Correction to `p5/P5_BUILD_REPORT.md` section 4 item 4: it says "against 100 um background cells"; the cells
there are 200 um (100 um applies only inside the lesion-path cones). The conclusion (mask feature + recipe,
not the construction; not repaired) stands; the decisive factors are the one-voxel neck, the extra narrowing by
smoothing (-18 % in r_eq) and the 200 um root cells off the lesion path.

Consequence for the batch (information for the analysis side, no action taken): any cohort tree with a
non-lesion branch narrower than about 2.5 root cells (inscribed diameter < ~0.5 mm) between the lesion path and
an outlet can lose that outlet the same way. The production recipe has no refinement on non-lesion branches;
the build gate "patches > 0 faces" catches it after the fact. A recipe change (e.g. a 100 um tube along every
branch, or a minimum-radius check on the smoothed surface before meshing) would be a recipe decision for the
analysis side, not a repair of this case.

## 2. "Final surface self-intersects at one point (inlet extension)": what it is

`surfaceCheck -checkSelfIntersection` on the final STL reports one location, (3.587, -195.366, 233.808) mm,
on the wall of the straight inlet flow extension, 5.13 mm upstream of the inlet plane at radius 1.94 mm
(the clipped loop's r_eq is 1.90 mm). Checks:

- An exact edge-triangle crossing test (double precision, after merging the STL vertices) over all non-adjacent
  triangle pairs with centroids within 0.6 mm (83 triangles) and within 3 mm (2,782 triangles) of the reported
  point finds **no crossing**. The triangles there are regular prism triangles (quality 0.77-0.85, edges
  150-290 um, minimum vertex spacing 4.9 um).
- `surfaceCheck` on the 3 mm sub-surface around the point: "Surface is not self-intersecting". On larger
  sub-surfaces (8, 20, 40 mm) it reports 2, 17 and 4 locations at *different* places; on the full surface the
  one original location. The reported location is not stable under the choice of the surface it is embedded in.

Interpretation: the flag is not a geometric self-intersection of the extension wall (a straight extrusion of
a planar simple loop, plane rms 2e-9 mm, cannot self-intersect) but a tolerance artefact of the
surfaceCheck intersection test (its tolerance depends on the surface's bounding box). The gate result
`surfaceCheck_not_self_intersecting = false` and the flag SELF_INTERSECTION stay as returned (the gate is defined
by the tool's verdict); this note qualifies it. It has no effect on the solve: cfMesh meshed through the
location, standard checkMesh is OK, and it is about 25 mm from the throat.

Limitation: the exact test covers 3 mm around the reported point, not the whole surface (the whole-surface
test was not run while the B2 timing measurement occupied the CPU).
