## 1. out_396 lost in the mesh: evidence-consistent mechanism

Each step below is measured. Localisation (added 19:17, after the B2 measurement): the wall patch of the mesh was
extracted (`surfaceMeshExtract`, 526,687 points) and every vertex of the final STL compared with it
(`localise272.py`, `localise272.json`). 754 STL vertices lie more than 0.3 mm from the meshed wall (maximum 7.5 mm):
751 of them on R-PDA tree nodes 381-396, i.e. from the neck (node 381) to the out_396 outlet and its extension
(517 at node 396), and 3 isolated single vertices elsewhere (at node 279 = the out_279 cap, node 620 = the out_620 cap,
node 0 = the inlet; single cap or extension vertices, not a region). The deleted region is therefore exactly the
R-PDA distal of the neck. That the neck's under-resolution is WHY cfMesh disconnected it remains the mechanism
consistent with all measurements (not tested by a re-mesh, which would be a recipe change):

1. **The mask lumen of the distal R-PDA is about two voxels wide, with a narrowing about 6.4 mm upstream of the
   outlet.** Voxel size 0.348 x 0.348 x 0.5 mm. At tree nodes 379-382 (arc 122.6-123.8 mm, 6.4 mm from the
   out_396 loop centroid) the EDT radius of the edited mask is 0.35-0.49 mm, i.e. one voxel; the package
   r_target is 0.42 mm (the tree's minimum r_target is at node 380).
2. **Marching cubes already pinches there, and the Taubin smoothing (30 iterations, passband 0.1, the 837
   recipe) narrows it further.** Section area-equivalent radius at node 381: raw marching cubes 0.339 mm ->
   smoothed 0.278 mm (-18 %), final STL 0.278 mm (inscribed radius 0.228 mm). Neighbouring nodes 380/382: raw
   0.430/0.381 -> smoothed 0.342/0.346 mm. Upstream and downstream of the neck (nodes 370-378, 383-392) raw and
   smoothed agree within about 0.04 mm. Smoothing shrinks this short, sharply curved one-voxel neck but not the
   regular vessel.
3. **No refinement object covers the neck, so cfMesh meshes it with its 200 um root cells.** meshDict:
   `maxCellSize 0.00020` (octree level 10, 0.2 mm); refinement objects are the lesion-path cones (40/70/140 um
   requests, radius 3 mm, on the RCA/R-PLA lesion path only) and 4 mm spheres at the inlet and outlet caps.
   Tree nodes 378-383 lie in none of them (`neck272.json` rows, field `refinement_objects_covering`, parsed from
   the meshDict: 104 objects); the out_396 cap sphere centre is 9.57 mm from node 381 (radius 4 mm). The inscribed diameter of the neck (0.456 mm) is therefore 2.3 root cells; its section
   area (0.243 mm2) is about six root-cell faces.
4. **cfMesh disconnects the region beyond the neck and deletes it.** `log.cartesianMesh`: "Mesh has 2 unconnected
   regions", "New cells size 5510458" after 5542985 (32,527 cells removed = the distal R-PDA with out_396),
   so out_396 has 0 faces.

Correction to `p5/P5_BUILD_REPORT.md` section 4 item 4: it says "against 100 um background cells"; the cells
there are 200 um (100 um applies only inside the lesion-path cones). The conclusion (mask feature + recipe,
not the construction; not repaired) stands; the measured contributing factors are the one-voxel neck, the extra
narrowing by smoothing (-18 % in r_eq) and the 200 um root cells off the lesion path.

Consequence for the batch (information for the analysis side, no action taken): this is one case, so no general
threshold is established; as a risk, a cohort tree whose non-lesion branch narrows to a few 200 um root cells between
the lesion path and an outlet may lose that outlet the same way. The production recipe has no refinement on non-lesion branches;
the build gate "patches > 0 faces" catches it after the fact. A recipe change (e.g. a 100 um tube along every
branch, or a minimum-radius check on the smoothed surface before meshing) would be a recipe decision for the
analysis side, not a repair of this case.

## 2. "Final surface self-intersects at one point (inlet extension)": what it is

`surfaceCheck -checkSelfIntersection` on the final STL reports one location, (3.587, -195.366, 233.808) mm,
on the wall of the straight inlet flow extension, 5.13 mm upstream of the inlet plane at radius 1.94 mm
(the clipped loop's r_eq is 1.90 mm). Checks:

- Edge-triangle crossing test (double precision, merged STL vertices) over the non-adjacent pairs with centroids
  within 0.6 mm (83 triangles) and 3 mm (2,782 triangles): no crossing (`selfint272_R*.json`).
- Triangle-triangle distance check (`tritri272.py`; triangles with any vertex within 3 mm: 2,930; exact minimum
  distance over the 6 vertex-triangle and 9 edge-edge distances plus a crossing test, so touching and coplanar
  overlap give 0; 9,739 non-adjacent pairs whose bounding boxes are within 50 um; edge-adjacent fold-overs and
  vertex-adjacent opposite-edge crossings also tested): minimum distance 4.85 um, no fold-over, no crossing. The
  4.85 um is the width of a column of sliver triangles that runs along the whole extension (two vertices of the
  clipped inlet loop 4.85 um apart, extruded ring by ring; quality 0.056) at azimuth 145 deg, about 0.6 mm from the
  reported point (azimuth 163 deg); the triangles at the reported point are regular (quality 0.77-0.85, edges
  150-290 um).
- `surfaceCheck` on the 3 mm sub-surface around the point: "Surface is not self-intersecting". On larger
  sub-surfaces (8, 20, 40 mm) it reports 2, 17 and 4 locations at *different* places; on the full surface the
  one original location (logs in `surfaceCheck_subsurface_logs/`). At three of the sub-surface locations the same
  triangle-triangle check (1 mm) finds no crossing either (minimum distances 14-22 um).

Interpretation: within the checked neighbourhoods no geometric intersection or contact exists, and the tool's
report moves with the surface it is embedded in. This is consistent with a tolerance artefact of the
surfaceCheck intersection test, but it is not proven (the cause inside the tool was not identified, and the
check covers neighbourhoods of the reported points, not the whole surface). The gate result
`surfaceCheck_not_self_intersecting = false` and the flag SELF_INTERSECTION stay as returned (the gate is defined
by the tool's verdict); this note qualifies it. It has no effect on the solve: cfMesh meshed through the
location, standard checkMesh is OK, and it is about 25 mm from the throat.

Open check: a whole-surface triangle-triangle check (only neighbourhoods of the reported points were checked).
The localisation of the deleted region, open in the first version, is done (section 1).
