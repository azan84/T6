## Verdict on the three M1 packages: **DO-NOT-SEND as currently generated → GO after two small fixes and a 3-second re-export**

The rewrite applied all eight MUSTs in intent, and six of them hold on the evidence. Two do not hold in the way that matters for Gate M1:

1. **The shipped flood-fill reference implementation, executed verbatim on the real scan-14 mask, no longer shreds the branch (0 islands) but erodes the vessel it is supposed to keep.** It removes **2061 voxels, not the 855 its own docstring quotes**; **1298 of them belong to the retained LAD** — a single 26-connected web of surface voxels spanning 18 × 31 × 38 mm, touching 97 of the 706 retained centreline points, 11–38 branch-radii away from the branch. The truncation step (same rule on `sub_cut_points_mm`) does the same to **every** M1 package, clean baseline included: 1787 voxels removed, 510 of them retained-tree erosion. Cause: the reviewer's probe used scipy's default **6-connectivity**; the exporter wrote **26-connectivity**, which walks the one-voxel shell that lies beyond 1.0 r of the centreline (27 420 of the mask's 42 707 voxels) into the trunk. Fix is one token (6-connectivity) plus one constant (protect 1.10 r); the scored table is in §MUST 3.
2. **The inlet normal is wrong by 32° in every package** (`inlet.json` and the `probes.csv` inlet row): `tangent(t, 0)` returns the placeholder `(0,0,1)` because the root has no parent, so the code substituted the tangent at the host vessel's first node — 11.4 mm downstream of the ostium (cos with the true root direction 0.85). The position is the ostium; the direction is the LAD's.

Neither is a design problem; both are in the rule text and in files the CFD operator will execute literally, and both would make M1 fail (pitted walls) or mis-build (inlet extension off-axis) for reasons that have nothing to do with real lumens. After the fix, re-export (`--m1`, 3 s) and send.

## Numbers that surprised me
- 26-connectivity vs 6-connectivity turns a correct rule (855 voxels, 92 stray) into one that removes **2061 voxels, 1298 of them from the vessel being kept**, with the mask still reporting "2 components" — the component count is not a sufficient acceptance test for a mask edit; a nearest-centreline FP/FN count is, and it costs 20 lines.
- **64 % of the mask's voxels lie beyond 1.0 r of every centreline point** — the MISR under-reads the mask lumen enough that "within 1.0 r" protects only the core.
- The measurement station is absent from 4 of 6 study-type packages I exported, by a 0.16 mm coin-flip in the merge order.
- The fixed minimiser changed **0 of 38** previously recorded Protocol C rows; the 10.1× and 2.95× rows are real single-basin optima.
