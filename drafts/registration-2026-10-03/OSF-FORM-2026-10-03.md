## 1. Study information

**Title**
Boundary-condition tuning and topological segmentation error in reduced-order coronary FFR: a pre-registered
three-protocol ablation at the 0.80 decision threshold

**Authors** (in order)
1. Fadillah Yamin — Department of Mechanical Engineering, School of Engineering, Monash University Malaysia
2. Mohd Azan Mohammed Sapardi — Department of Mechanical Engineering, Kulliyyah of Engineering, International Islamic
   University Malaysia — ORCID 0000-0001-5278-014X
3. Ming Kwang Tan — Department of Mechanical Engineering, School of Engineering, Monash University Malaysia; and
   Department of Mechanical Engineering, Ming Chi University of Technology, Taiwan — ORCID 0000-0002-1585-9358
4. Xin Wang — Department of Mechanical Engineering, School of Engineering, Monash University Malaysia —
   ORCID 0000-0002-5854-5287
5. Mohd-Zulhilmi Paiz Ismadi (corresponding) — Department of Mechanical Engineering, School of Engineering, Monash
   University Malaysia — ORCID 0000-0002-7724-114X

**Description**
Image-based fractional flow reserve (FFR) models are tuned so that their outlet flows match perfusion targets. This
study tests whether that tuning conceals segmentation errors, so that a model matches its validation targets while the
FFR ≤ 0.80 revascularisation decision changes. Four segmentation error types are injected into real coronary trees
from the public ImageCAS-X dataset: two topological (a missed side branch, a vessel break) and two calibre (stenosis
length, taper). Each corrupted tree is solved under three boundary-condition protocols: A, fixed (the clean tree's
boundary conditions kept); B, re-derived by the deployment rule from the corrupted tree; and C, re-tuned to match the
clean tree's territory perfusion. The outcome is the per-instance change in FFR 20 mm distal to the lesion and
whether the 0.80 decision flips, judged against the instance's own clean baseline and compared with a
physiological-noise floor. A steady 3D CFD arm on a 30-instance subset tests whether the reduced-order result
transfers. A detector built only from outputs the pipeline already computes tests whether concealed error can be
found at deployment.

**Hypotheses** (each registered with its counter-hypothesis; both directions are publishable results)

| # | Hypothesis | Counter-hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Topological errors (T1, T2) produce a higher P(flip) than calibre errors (T3, T4) | Calibre errors dominate |
| H2 | Protocol C drives the territory-perfusion residual below the validation threshold while P(flip) stays ≥ Protocol A (absorption) | Protocol C also reduces P(flip) (tuning rescues) |
| H3 | Protocol B yields P(flip) ≥ Protocol A | B ≈ A |
| H4 | 0D and 3D flip patterns agree, κ > 0.6 | They disagree; 0D findings do not transfer |
| H5 | Detector AUC > 0.80 on a held-out split and > 0.70 externally | The detector fails; absorption leaves no deployment-time signature |
| H6 | The downstream-branch-loss effect is large under fixed resistance and small under re-tuned resistance | The effect does not depend on protocol |

H7 (paired-anatomy arm) is a planned amendment (`STATISTICS-PLAN.md` §13), not part of this registration.

## 2. Design plan

**Study type:** Other — computational simulation study on a public dataset. There is no human-subject contact.

**Blinding:** No blinding in the 0D arm (deterministic computation). In the 3D arm, the CFD operator receives case
packages without 0D predictions and returns results before the 0D predictions for those instances are released
(`CFD-ARM-SPEC.md` §13).

**Study design:** Within-instance factorial: error type (4) × boundary-condition protocol (3) × bed structure (2:
distributed-leak "leaky" bed and discrete-outlet bed). Every instance is its own control. Full specification:
`SEVERITY-SWEEP-SPEC.md` (lesion insertion, eligibility, factors), `CFD-ARM-SPEC.md` v0.2 §2 (protocols, beds) and
`code/error_types.py` (error definitions and magnitudes).

**Randomisation:** None. The cohort is a frozen, hashed instance list, not a seed (`STATISTICS-PLAN.md` §11).

## 3. Sampling plan

**Existing data:** Registration prior to analysis of the data. The dataset is public and in hand. Baseline
(uncorrupted) FFR was computed to build the cohort. No ablation result has been computed on the cohort except the
instances listed in the addendum, §A3 (8 instances, seen during code verification and the 3D gate). They are
disclosed with what was computed and when, and a sensitivity analysis excludes them.

**Explanation of existing data:** ImageCAS-X annotation layer (centrelines, voxel segmentations, surfaces,
descriptors), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21887809, version 1 (11 August 2026), CC BY 4.0. Baselines for the prevalence
check (E0) and the severity sweep were computed on 2026-09-18 to select the cohort. All 93 cohort patients are in the
dataset's held-out test split.

**Data collection procedures:** Virtual stenoses (cosine profile; 40–80 %DS; 10 or 20 mm; proximal or mid) are
inserted into eligible host vessels. The host must have r ≥ 1.0 mm at the lesion centre, a resolved measurement node
20 mm distal to the lesion, native disease < 40 %DS and baseline FFR ≥ 0.90. Each instance is then corrupted by
each error type. See `SEVERITY-SWEEP-SPEC.md`.

**Sample size:** 150 instances (LAD/LCx/RCA 50 each) from 108 trees and 93 patients
(`COHORT-FROZEN-2026-09-18.csv`). The discrete-bed arm admits 97 of these. The 3D subset has 30 instances, 10 per
vessel, one per tree (`CFD-SUBSET-FROZEN-2026-09-18.csv`).

**Sample size rationale:** Set by cohort supply. With 150 paired instances and a patient-level random effect, a paired
difference in flip probability of about 12 percentage points is detectable at 80 % power, α = 0.05
(`STATISTICS-PLAN.md` §8). The discrete arm and the 3D arm are powered for direction only.

**Stopping rule:** Not applicable. The cohort is fixed.

## 4. Variables

**Manipulated variables:** error type (T1 missed side branch; T2 vessel break at 25 mm; T3 stenosis-length error;
T4 taper, radius × 0.930); protocol (A, B, C); bed structure (leaky, discrete). Magnitudes are constants in
`code/error_types.py`. T3 and T4 are derived from published inter-observer statistics. T1 and T2 are declared
design choices with no measured magnitude.

**Measured variables:** FFR at the measurement node (pressure / aortic pressure, P_aorta = 90 mmHg); the
territory-perfusion residual against the clean tree's full territory outflow; per-outlet flow and pressure; fit
diagnostics for Protocol C.

**Indices:** ΔFFR = FFR(corrupted) − FFR(clean); flip = 1 if the 0.80 classification differs from the clean
baseline's; "passes validation" = territory-perfusion residual < 10 % (sensitivity analyses at 13 % and 16 %);
"materially wrong" = |ΔFFR| > 0.05.

## 5. Analysis plan

**Statistical models:** See `STATISTICS-PLAN.md` §4 (attached whole). In summary:
P1, a mixed-effects logistic regression `flip ~ error_type * protocol + ds_pct + L_mm + band + (1 | scan/slot)`
per bed structure, with paired protocol contrasts by McNemar's exact test and a paired mixed model for ΔFFR.
P2, the proportion of instances that pass validation and are materially wrong, per protocol, with Wilson intervals.
P3, a claim is made only where direction agrees in both bed structures. P4 (H4), Cohen's κ and Bland–Altman on the
3D subset, with the as-meshed-radius twin as the primary comparison (addendum §A2) and the pre-registered contingency
on U₃D. P5 (H5), ROC with DeLong confidence intervals on a held-out split and then an external dataset.

**Transformations:** None beyond the indices above. Bands of baseline FFR are re-derived per bed structure.

**Inference criteria:** Holm–Bonferroni across H1–H6, family-wise α = 0.05. Protocol contrasts within a hypothesis
are Holm-corrected within family. No flip rate is reported without the physiological-noise floor beside it
(`STATISTICS-PLAN.md` §6). An effect that does not exceed the floor is reported as "not distinguishable from
physiological noise".

**Data exclusion:** Rule-based and logged with reasons (`STATISTICS-PLAN.md` §9). Protocol C requires at least two
clean-partition territories with surviving members, and a fit at a search bound is a failed fit. This removes
Protocol C from 40 % of T1 rows and 33 % of T2 rows, almost all RCA. The tuned-protocol arm is therefore a
left-coronary result for the topological error types, and this is declared in advance.

**Missing data:** Non-converged solves and 3D meshing failures are reported as outcomes, never dropped silently.

**Exploratory analysis:** Interactions of image quality with error type; anything not specified in
`STATISTICS-PLAN.md` or the addendum is labelled exploratory, in those words.
