# Suggested reviewers, JBHI submission (2026-10-08)

JBHI encourages four independent reviewers from different institutions. Selection rule used: an expert whose own
published findings this paper confirms, extends or builds on, with no commercial stake in computed FFR, no known
co-authorship or collaboration with any of the five authors, and no prior correspondence with the authors about this
study. Guest editors (M. Colombo, S. Celi, D. Marlevi, M. Sigovan) are excluded. Before entering them, confirm each
has had no joint paper, grant or supervision link with any author in the last five years.
